## A · Badge photos for the staff directory

The in-game company directory shows each person's **ID badge photo, taken in their first
week at the company** (the year is given below). Some are many years old, so the person
looks younger and styled for that year. The game says so openly ("the photo does not
change; people do").

Style for all: plain corporate ID photo. Head and shoulders, facing the camera, neutral
mild smile or neutral face, evenly lit with a slightly flat on-camera flash, plain
light blue-grey backdrop, square 1024x1024. No text, no lanyard text, no logos, no
borders. Make the older photos look their age: a 2008 photo is a little softer and flatter
than a 2021 one, with that year's hair and clothes. Fictional people only; no resemblance
to real public figures.

## Check before you finish

Look at every image. Regenerate if: Grace is not recognisably the sheet's Grace at a
younger age; anyone else resembles a recurring character; any readable text, logo, or
brand appears; a badge photo looks like a modern selfie instead of an ID photo of its
year. Write `NOTES.md`: each file, its final prompt, and anything you could not get right.
Do not edit any other file.
