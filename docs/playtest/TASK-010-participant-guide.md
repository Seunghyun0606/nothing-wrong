# Nothing Wrong playtest: participant guide

This session tests the game, not you. You play a new QA engineer at a testing company.
Work arrives in Messenger. You test a fake shop and, later, a client's records, and you
report what does not match the requirements. Along the way you piece together what is
going on behind the work. Exploring costs you nothing. A report the team sends back, or
a wrong answer to the team's question, adds 20 minutes to the workday; you can always try
again. (The clock also moves as messages arrive, so it may jump by more than 20.)
Please say aloud what you expect, notice, or find confusing as you play.

## Start

The facilitator starts the game on a fresh save. On the title screen, choose **Start
work** (업무 시작), then **Sign in** (로그인) on the lock screen; the access code is already
filled in. The desktop has three apps:

- **Messenger** (메신저): the team's channels and direct messages. Tickets arrive here.
- **Browser** (브라우저): the shop, the intranet, and the company directory.
- **QA Notes** (QA 노트): your Tickets, your notes, and the Case File (사건 파일).

**Arrange** (화면 정렬) on the taskbar puts the Browser on the left and QA Notes on the
right. When QA Notes is narrow, pick a Ticket from the list at its top and scroll for
the rest of the page.

The shop and the client's record screens are in English on purpose, even when the game
is in Korean: they are the client's own websites.

Please do not read the repository's docs or tests before or during the session. They
contain the answers.

## Playing

When a notification comes in at the bottom right, choose **Open** (열기) and read the briefing.
In QA Notes, choose **Start test** (테스트 시작) on the Ticket, then test in the Browser.
If something does not match the requirements, press **📌 Note** (📌 메모) and click the
highlighted part. To report it, open the report form in Messenger, pick your note and the
requirement it breaks, and choose **Send report** (보고 보내기). If everything holds after
checking the screen the Ticket is about, choose **Nothing is wrong** (이상 없음), then
**Send NO ISSUE FOUND**. Afterwards the team may ask you to look something up and answer a
question. At the end of a workday a card at the bottom right offers **Clock out**
(퇴근하기), and the next day you sign in again.

If you get stuck, try the "Stuck? Ask … for a hint" link (막혔나요? … 힌트 묻기) on the
Ticket in QA Notes first; it may be further down the page. Then tell the facilitator what
you were trying to do before asking them for help.

You do not have to finish the game. The facilitator will say when the session ends.

## After playing

Answer these six questions in your own words. Examples from particular moments are more
useful than a score alone. It is fine to answer "no" or "not sure".

1. 이슈를 찾는 과정이 재미있는가?
2. Shoply가 믿을 수 있는 서비스처럼 느껴지는가?
3. 정상 동작과 결함을 구분할 수 있는가?
4. 재현과 Evidence 확보가 만족스러운가?
5. Report가 서류 작업이 아니라 Gameplay로 느껴지는가?
6. 다음 Ticket을 열고 싶은가?

Optional, if there is time:

- 추리 질문이 찾은 단서로 풀 수 있을 만큼 공정했는가?
- 하루가 끝나고 다음 날로 넘어가는 흐름이 자연스러웠는가?

The facilitator may ask which screen or moment led to each answer. The project owner,
not the participant, decides what happens to the game next.
