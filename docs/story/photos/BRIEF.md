# Photo brief: "Nothing Wrong" story photos

You are producing the in-game photographs for "Nothing Wrong", a quiet corporate-mystery
game. The player is a QA engineer who tests a big company's migrated records and slowly
notices that the people in old company photos knew each other for years. The photos must
look like **ordinary corporate PR and event photographs** that a company CMS would hold:
competent, slightly flat, a little over-lit, nothing dramatic. The unease comes only from
who is standing next to whom. Never make anything look sinister, violent, or staged like
a thriller.

Use your image generation tool. Save every result in this directory as PNG with exactly
the file names below (copy the generated file here; overwrite if you regenerate). Work
in the order given: generate the two reference sheets first, then pass the character
sheet (and the emblem sheet where the emblem appears) as a reference image for every
scene so faces and the emblem stay the same in all photos. Face consistency matters more
than anything else: the player has to recognise the same four people across photos taken
years apart.

## Global art direction

- Photorealistic documentary event photography, natural skin, no glamour retouching,
  realistic camera flash or venue lighting, slight lens softness, 35 mm look.
- Muted, slightly cool palette (greys, navy, warm skin tones, small touches of gold for
  the Foundation). It must sit well inside a calm blue-grey desktop UI.
- Landscape 3:2 (1536x1024) unless stated otherwise.
- Fictional people only. Do not resemble any real celebrity or public figure. No real
  company logos or brand names. No watermarks, no captions, no UI, no frames.
- Background text: avoid all readable text **except** the exact banner words given for a
  photo. If the model cannot render a given banner text cleanly, leave the banner with
  just the emblem rather than garbled letters. No other signs, name tags, or slides with
  legible words (keep slide/screen content abstract: charts, shapes).
- No weapons, injuries, blood, alcohol-heavy party scenes, or anything that suggests a
  crime. Adults only.

## The recurring people (same face in every photo)

- **Daniel Park**: Korean-American man, early 30s in 2021 (late 20s in 2019), slim, short
  black hair neatly parted, thin rectangular glasses, earnest friendly face, light stubble
  never. Wears a navy suit with no tie at events.
- **Grace Yoon**: Korean-American woman, early 40s, shoulder-length dark brown hair with a
  side part, composed confident smile, small pearl earrings. Usually a charcoal-grey
  tailored suit.
- **Samuel Kwon**: Korean-American man, early 60s, tall (a head taller than the others),
  broad shoulders, silver swept-back hair, deep-set eyes, warm public smile. Always wears a
  small gold **sun-in-a-ring** lapel pin (see emblem) on a dark suit.
- **Horizon CEO** (no name shown): white American man, mid 50s, short sandy-grey hair,
  clean-shaven, square jaw, light-blue shirt and dark suit, also wears the gold
  sun-in-a-ring pin when Samuel is present.
- **Foundation security director**: Hispanic American man, mid 40s, stocky, close-cropped
  dark hair, short beard, serious neutral expression, dark-green field jacket with a small
  sleeve patch of the emblem and a clip-on badge (do not render readable badge text).

## The Horizon Foundation emblem

A benign charity logo: a half sun rising (semicircle with seven short rays) inside a thin
complete ring. Gold on navy, or navy on white. It should read as a friendly community
foundation, never occult or threatening.
